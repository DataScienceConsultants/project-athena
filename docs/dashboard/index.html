INTERPRETATION

What v5 supports — and what it does not

Strongest pattern

Large source earthquakes are followed by a strong short-term concentration of nearby M6+ events.

Distance behavior

The descriptive excess weakens sharply with distance and approaches baseline at larger distances.

Boundary metric

PB2002 along-boundary distance mostly reorganizes the same nearby sequences rather than exposing a new distant population.

Not established

No causal triggering, stress transfer, energy transfer, rupture propagation, statistical significance, or future-earthquake probability is claimed.
